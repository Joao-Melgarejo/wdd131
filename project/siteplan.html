Site Name

The Master

I chose this name because it is the real name of the business. Many local people also know it as “Gasfitero Fernando”, because Fernando is the owner and the person who does the service. I think this name is simple and matches the identity of the business.

Optional domain: themasterplumbing.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to give information about a local plumbing business with more than 15 years of experience. The site will show the main services, contact information, and basic details for local customers. It will help people know who Fernando is, what kind of work he does, and how they can contact the business.

Scenarios

  • What plumbing services does Fernando offer?
  • How can I contact The Master for a home service?
  • Does this business have experience in this kind of work?
  • Can I quickly see the business information before I call?

Color Schema

I chose these colors because they match the business image and also look clear on a website. Blue will be the main color because it gives trust and a professional look. Red will be used as a strong accent color because it is already visible in the logo. A light background will help the text and sections look clean and easy to read.

Blue — #1D4E89

Used for headings, navigation, borders, and important titles.

Red — #E53935

Used for buttons, highlights, and call to action sections.

Light Gray — #F5F5F5

Used for page background and content areas.

Dark Gray — #333333

Used for normal text because it is easier to read.

Typography

I chose simple fonts that look clean and are easy to read. They can work well for both desktop and mobile view.

Montserrat

Used for headings, section titles, and navigation.

Roboto

Used for paragraphs, lists, and general information.
